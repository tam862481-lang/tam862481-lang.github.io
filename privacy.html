Turbo File Transfer Desktop

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy describes how Turbo File Transfer Desktop handles information when you use the desktop application.

Information the application accesses

When you choose to use Google Drive features, the application requests Google OAuth access for the feature you selected. Download mode requests read-only access to your Drive so it can list and download files that you choose. Upload mode requests the narrower Drive file access needed to upload files to a Drive location you authorize.

The application may process Google account authorization tokens, Drive file names and metadata, file contents involved in a transfer, and the local file or folder paths and transfer settings you choose.

How information is used and shared

The application uses this information only to perform transfers and related actions that you start in the application. Requests are sent directly from your device to Google OAuth and Google Drive services. The application developer does not operate a server that receives your Drive files or OAuth tokens. The application does not include advertising or analytics, and the developer does not sell or share your Drive data.

Google processes information sent to its services under Google's own privacy terms. Your use of Google Drive remains subject to Google's terms and privacy policy.

Storage and retention on your device

OAuth tokens are stored locally and protected with the operating system's credential protection: Windows DPAPI on Windows or macOS Keychain-backed encryption on macOS. The application also stores local preferences and transfer state, which can include paths you selected, queue entries, and diagnostic logs. Transfer files remain in the local folder or Google Drive location you selected.

Local information remains on your device until you remove it. You can use the application's disconnect control to delete its locally saved token. This does not revoke Google's authorization by itself; you can separately revoke the application's access from your Google Account security settings. You can delete other local application data by removing the application's user data folder.

Security

The application uses HTTPS for Google OAuth and Drive API requests and stores OAuth tokens using operating-system credential protection. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so keep your device and Google Account protected.

Children

This desktop utility is not designed specifically for children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's data handling changes. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.
