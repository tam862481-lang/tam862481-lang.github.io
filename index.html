Turbo File Transfer Desktop

A desktop utility for transfers that you start between your Google Drive and your computer.

What the application does

The application lets you sign in to Google and download Drive files to a folder you choose, or upload local files to a Drive location you authorize. Transfers are started and controlled by you in the desktop application.

Google sign-in is required to access private Drive data. Download and upload access are requested separately and only when you use the corresponding feature.

Privacy

The application communicates directly from your device with Google OAuth and Google Drive services. The developer does not operate a server that receives your Google Drive files or OAuth tokens. The application does not include advertising or analytics.

Read how the application handles information in the Privacy Policy.
